about=MyQgisVM is a comprehensive GIS data processing toolkit for Vietnamese urban planning standards:
    - Assign VN-2000 coordinate system (3-degree zone) by province and attach GeoMap basemap
    - Auto scan and fix geometry errors: Invalid Geometry, Self-Intersection, Spikes, Duplicates, Topology Overlap
    - Advanced Polygon processing: remove slivers, trim narrow branches, replace geometry, coordinate lookup
    - Attribute management and QPK standardization: add 6 mandatory fields, bulk fill values, auto-calculate area/length
    - Data merge and conversion: export to DXF, KML, GDB, Shapefile, GeoPackage
    - Package spatial database following Circular 16/2025/TT-BXD standard (HoSoGIS, Feature Dataset, Feature Class)
